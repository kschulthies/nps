Scoring choices and transparency

Chosen boundary

5 stars = Promoter; 4 stars = Passive; 1–3 stars = Detractor. A 5-star-only promoter rule reserves the strongest loyalty label for the highest rating. A 4-star review signals a positive experience, but not unqualified advocacy, so it remains Passive. The alternate score moves 4-star reviews to Promoters and shows the sensitivity.

Source and selection

Source: Trustpilot for all five companies. The workbook contains the 60 most recent reviews captured for each company. The workbook contains 60 collected reviews per company. Profile totals can be entered on the company cards to show how large each sample is relative to the reviews listed on Trustpilot. The date span needed to collect 60 reviews also varies and serves as a recent-activity proxy.

Selection bias: People who choose to post on Trustpilot may have unusually good or bad experiences, so these reviews may not represent all customers.
